## Standard purpose

Collect all café/Workbar serviceware that has migrated to office-floor kitchens and restore the full Workbar default before the task is considered complete.

## Equipment and separation

- Use one trolley.
- Use at least two empty trays: one clean tray and one dirty tray.
- Keep clean and dirty items separated throughout the route; never mix them in the same tray.

## Exact route

1. Start in Workbar.
2. Take the Workbar elevator to floor 2.
3. Inspect both kitchens on floor 2, beginning on the Workbar-elevator side and finishing at the other elevator.
4. Take the other elevator to floor 3.
5. Inspect both kitchens on floor 3, moving from the other-elevator side toward the Workbar elevator.
6. Take the Workbar elevator to floor 4.
7. Inspect both kitchens on floor 4, moving from the Workbar-elevator side toward the other elevator.
8. Take the other elevator to floor 5 and inspect the kitchen on that side.
9. Never pass through the office that separates the two sides of floor 5.
10. Take the other elevator back to floor 4, return to the Workbar elevator without repeating the completed kitchen inspection, and take the Workbar elevator to floor 5.
11. Inspect the kitchen closest to the Workbar elevator.
12. Return to Workbar using the Workbar elevator.

## Scope

- Floors 2, 3, 4 and 5.
- Exactly two kitchens per floor: one large kitchen and one small kitchen; eight kitchens total.
- No office areas are part of the inspection.
- The office separating the two sides of floor 5 is explicitly excluded.

## Collection and sorting

Collect cappuccino cups, espresso cups, cocktail glasses, wine glasses, beer glasses, coffee cups, other glassware or serviceware belonging to Workbar/Café, and any such equipment present in excess of what belongs on the office floor.

- Clean Workbar/Café items go directly to their correct shelf or fixed place.
- Dirty items go to the prep kitchen, are washed, and are then put in their correct place.
- Clean surplus not needed in active service goes to the correct prep-kitchen storage.
- Clean and dirty items must never be mixed in the same tray.

## Timing, responsibility and completion

- Opening: perform the route at any appropriate point and complete it no later than 10:45.
- Closing/daily recovery: perform it after lunch service and staff lunch; the normal target is approximately 13:30.
- The responsible person for the shift performs the route. It is designed for one person.
- Completion requires the full configured Workbar default stock of cups, glasses and relevant serviceware to be physically restored. Completing the walk alone is insufficient.
- Missing equipment must be found; dirty equipment must be washed; all required equipment must be returned to its correct position. The Opening worker must arrive to a fully prepared Workbar.

## Unavailable floor or kitchen

Do not wait and do not bypass access control. Notify the manager, record the specific inaccessible area as a deviation, continue the remainder of the route, and keep the Workbar default-restoration requirement in force.

## Reference and editability policy

No image or route diagram is mandatory, and this amendment creates no logical image blocker. The sequence and instructions remain editable through the manager standard and task editors. No image is fabricated or uploaded by this amendment.

## Task allocation

- C03 executes the shared route after lunch service and staff lunch, normally around 13:30.
- C27 reviews the shared C03 route evidence and completes final serviceware/layout accountability; it does not force an unnecessary second route.
