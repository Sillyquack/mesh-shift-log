# Mesh Visual Standards — image preparation and upload checklist

Use this list before and during the production image upload. It is intentionally practical: every uploaded image should answer **“What must the finished result look like?”** without requiring a second explanation.

## Universal image rules

### Before taking the photo

- Complete the setup to the real operational standard first.
- Remove personal belongings, cleaning clutter and temporary delivery packaging.
- Remove names, phone numbers, booking details, QR codes and customer information.
- Never photograph alarm codes, PINs, access cards, passwords or keys in identifiable positions.
- Do not include guests or colleagues unless their presence is essential and explicit consent is documented.
- Check that fire exits, accessible routes and safety equipment are not presented incorrectly.
- Use the same real equipment and furniture staff will handle.

### Framing and quality

- Prefer landscape orientation for room layouts and stations.
- Use portrait only when the standard is genuinely vertical, such as a fridge or narrow equipment panel.
- Photograph from the natural approach angle staff will recognize.
- Include enough surroundings to make the location obvious.
- Avoid extreme wide-angle distortion.
- Ensure labels and relevant controls are readable.
- Use steady focus and sufficient light; do not upload dark or blurry images.
- Show one unambiguous finished state per image.
- Retake the image when a temporary substitution makes the standard misleading.

### File rules

- Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG or WebP.
- Maximum file size: 5 MB.
- Use a local permanent file, not a signed Notion, Slack, email or temporary cloud URL.
- Do not edit the file extension to disguise another format; file content is validated.
- Keep the original file until production verification is complete.

### Metadata rules

Every actual image requires:

- **Name:** short operational label, for example “Atrium café”.
- **What this image proves:** the standard the image demonstrates.
- **Image description:** meaningful alt text describing the completed setup.
- **Caption:** optional context that helps without repeating the title.

Good image description:

> Atrium café layout with round tables evenly spaced, clear central guest route and café counter unobstructed.

Weak image description:

> Photo of Atrium.

### Upload verification

After every upload:

1. Open the image from Operations Studio.
2. Confirm orientation, crop, description and caption.
3. Open the same visual guide from Julie’s Event Mode.
4. Confirm only the current version appears.
5. Confirm the written checklist still makes sense beside the image.
6. Keep the slot as an honest placeholder if the image is not good enough.

## Required event image set

### Atrium — furniture and room rigs

| Stable key | Display label | Image must prove |
|---|---|---|
| `atrium-cafe` | Cafe | Approved café furniture placement, guest route and clear café area |
| `atrium-classroom` | Classroom | Correct classroom rows, table spacing, front orientation and access routes |
| `atrium-parking-lot` | Parking Lot | Approved Parking Lot arrangement and clear operational flow |
| `atrium-cinema-cafe` | Cinema around cafe | Cinema seating facing the café side with correct aisle and front space |
| `atrium-cinema-stage` | Cinema around stage | Cinema seating facing the stage with correct aisle and front space |
| `atrium-empty` | Empty Atrium | What “empty and ready” means, including where furniture is no longer present |
| `atrium-standing` | Standing Event | Standing furniture, mingling zones and unobstructed guest flow |
| `atrium-cocktail` | Cocktail | Approved cocktail tables, mingling layout and service routes |

Quality check: photograph the full room from a repeatable corner whenever possible. The image must show exits and primary guest flow clearly enough to prevent overfilling the room.

### Atrium — popup bar

| Stable key | Display label | Image must prove |
|---|---|---|
| `atrium-bar-ready` | Atrium bar ready | Correct stock, glassware, ice, tools, devices and clean working surface before service |
| `atrium-bar-closed` | Atrium bar closed | Final cleaned, restocked and correctly stored bar after service |

Quality check: open or perishable products must follow the approved procedure at the moment the photo is taken. Do not accidentally present an unsafe overnight standard.

### Atrium — serving stations

| Stable key | Display label | Image must prove |
|---|---|---|
| `atrium-drinks-under-25` | Drinks — under 25 | Small drinks station sized for fewer than 25 guests |
| `atrium-drinks-over-25` | Drinks — over 25 | Larger drinks station with sufficient capacity and queue space |

| `atrium-wine-beer` | Wine and beer | Approved self-service or hosted wine/beer station and glassware |

Quality check: allergen information must be visible where relevant, but no customer or booking name should appear in the photograph.

### Atrium — stage and hybrid technology

| Stable key | Display label | Image must prove |
|---|---|---|
| `atrium-stage-tech-overview` | Complete default setup | Full stage, screen, soundbar and hybrid-equipment default |
| `atrium-hdmi-inputs` | HDMI inputs | Both available HDMI inputs and correct cable routing |

| `atrium-clicker-batteries` | Clicker and batteries | Charged clicker and correct spare batteries in approved location |

Quality check: photograph equipment placement, not credentials, serial-number records or private network information. The setup must have been functionally tested before it is called the standard.

### Cornerbar

| Stable key | Display label | Image must prove |
|---|---|---|
| `cornerbar-event-ready` | Cornerbar event-ready | Correct furniture, signs, devices, stations, stock and guest flow |
| `cornerbar-bar-ready` | Cornerbar bar ready | Correct stock, ice, glassware, tools, devices and clean surface |
| `cornerbar-final-reset` | Cornerbar final reset | Chairs on tables, devices charging, fridges off, labels removed, bottles handled and fruit discarded |

Quality check: the final-reset image must not reveal an alarm code or imply that a security step has been completed when it has not.

### Shared coffee, tea and water standards

| Stable key | Display label | Image must prove |
|---|---|---|
| `coffee-tea-complete` | Complete station | Finished cups, plates, spoons, milk, sugar, napkins, labels, water and waste setup |
| `coffee-tea-refill` | Refill stock | Correct hidden refill quantities and storage position |

Quality check: the complete station is guest-facing; the refill image should make the storage location unmistakable without exposing unrelated stock or personal items.

### Food, buffet and allergen standards

| Stable key | Display label | Image must prove |
|---|---|---|
| `food-main` | Food service | Main food-station layout, service tools, plates and guest flow |
| `food-snacks` | Snacks and pastry | Approved snack and pastry presentation |
| `food-cheese-jam` | Cheese and jam | Cheese, jam and accompaniment arrangement |
| `food-allergens` | Allergen signs | Clearly visible allergen labels with allergens emphasized |

Quality check: photograph the real sign format staff must reproduce. Confirm the allergen wording is current before using the image as a standard.

## Known intentional gaps

The current Workbar Photos page in Notion contains no completed updated image set. Do not invent Workbar standards to make the progress indicator reach 100%. Existing working standards stay in force until reviewed Workbar images are supplied.

CommunityStage and LoungeVenue also have written guides without a new Julie image set. These remain text-only unless a manager deliberately creates and reviews new visual standards.

## Recommended upload order

1. Cornerbar final reset and bar ready.
2. Atrium bar ready and closed.
3. Atrium stage / technology.
4. Coffee, tea, water and used dishes.
5. Food and allergen stations.
6. Atrium room rigs.
7. Secondary and less frequently used station variants.

This order prioritizes the standards most likely to prevent operational mistakes, product loss or an incomplete close.
