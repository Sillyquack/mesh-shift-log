# Mesh Visual Standards — image preparation and upload checklist

Use this list before any separately authorized production image upload. The canonical Venue → Guide → Zone → Angle queue, stable keys, suggested filenames and proof text are generated in [`event-visual-library-upload-manifest.md`](./event-visual-library-upload-manifest.md). Do not maintain a second handwritten key list here.

## Before taking a photo

- Complete the setup to the real operational standard first.
- Remove personal belongings, cleaning clutter and temporary delivery packaging.
- Remove names, phone numbers, booking details, QR codes and customer information.
- Never photograph alarm codes, PINs, access cards, passwords or identifiable key positions.
- Do not include people unless their presence is essential and explicit consent is documented.
- Check that exits, accessible routes and safety equipment are presented correctly.
- Use the same real equipment and furniture staff will handle.

## Framing and quality

- Follow the angle role and operational proof in the generated manifest; distinct angles are not duplicates.
- Prefer landscape for rooms and stations; use portrait only for a genuinely vertical target.
- Photograph from the repeatable approach that staff will recognize.
- Include enough surroundings to make the zone and circulation clear.
- Avoid extreme wide-angle distortion, dark exposures and unreadable labels.
- Show one unambiguous finished state per image.
- Retake an image when a temporary substitution makes the standard misleading.

## Files and metadata

- Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG or WebP, maximum 5 MB.
- Use a local permanent file, never a signed Notion, chat, email or temporary cloud URL.
- Keep the original until production verification is complete.
- Use the suggested filename from the manifest where practical.
- Supply a short operational label, “what this proves”, meaningful alt text, and optional caption.
- Alt text must describe the completed target, not merely say “photo of” the room.

## Safety review before upload

- Inspect the full-resolution file for credentials, access details, personal data and unintended customer information.
- Confirm the image matches the selected default or customer layout; never substitute one for the other.
- Confirm the operational facts beside the guide are current.
- For Atrium Stage Tech, the reviewed current target is 2 handheld and 2 headset microphones. Do not use the superseded three-headset / throwable-microphone copy.
- For Cornerbar, furniture restoration and bar-closing proof remain separate journeys.
- Do not invent a Workbar image: the updated source is empty and its written standard remains in force.

## Verification after each authorized upload

1. Open the image from Operations Studio.
2. Confirm stable key, guide, zone, angle, orientation, crop, alt text and caption.
3. Open the same guide from Julie’s Event Mode.
4. Confirm only the current version appears and older versions remain auditable.
5. Confirm the written reconstruction still makes sense beside the image.
6. Return the reference to an honest placeholder if the image is wrong, unsafe or inaccessible.

For this release-preparation branch, every image remains `awaiting production upload`; no production upload is authorized by this checklist.
